**Don’t Hold Me Back** sees **MINIJACK** teaming up with **Emél** and **EL 360** for a deep house track built around groove and mood.

Driven by a steady rhythm and clean, understated production, the track leaves room to breathe — letting both the vocal and the atmosphere do the work. Emél’s performance sits front and center, while EL 360’s spoken word adds depth and character, together pulling the listener in.

Since release, the track has passed 200,000 streams and continues to find its way into new sets and playlists.
