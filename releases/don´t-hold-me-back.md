**Don't Hold Me Back** is an emotional deep house journey. MINIJACK, Emél, and EL 360 came together to create something that hits you right in the feels.

Emél's voice carries the weight of the lyrics — about breaking free, moving forward, not letting anything stop you. The production builds around her, with layers of synths and a bassline that pulses with determination.

EL 360 adds his unique touch, bringing an edge that makes this track stand out. It's deep house with heart, and it resonates.
