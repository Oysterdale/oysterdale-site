**Roadtrip To Brazil** sees **Sebastian Emes** heading south for a sun-drenched dose of disco house, featuring **Bruna Santana** on vocals.

Born and raised in São Paulo, Bruna brings authenticity, emotion and elegance to the record with her soulful Portuguese vocals. In 2022, she was awarded "Best Brazilian Singer in Europe" by High Profile Magazine in Paris.

Built around a classic groove and bright, warm production, the track channels that feeling of open roads, coastal air, and somewhere warm waiting at the end of the line. Uplifting groove and organic instrumentation come together in a track designed for sunsets, open-air parties and late summer nights.

Every record is a pearl.
