**Andromeda** marks the debut release from Norwegian drummer and producer Ekelove. Blending live drums and percussion with warm analogue synths and carefully programmed elements, the track moves seamlessly between the organic and the electronic. Rhythmic details unfold throughout, creating a hypnotic groove and an atmospheric, ever-evolving soundscape. With “Andromeda,” Ekelove introduces a distinctive musical universe built on live musicianship, electronic textures and a deep sense of movement.
