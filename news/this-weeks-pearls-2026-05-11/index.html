This Week's Pearls – 2026-05-11

by Oysterdale Records

house disco weekly-brief pearls

Been digging through the crates again this week, and the groove is strong. Spring is fully here in Oslo, basement sessions are stretching later into the evening, and the new releases are matching that energy perfectly.

Route 94's return to Defected feels significant – "4Get The Girl" has that rolling, peak-time energy that made his name a decade ago, but with a maturity that comes from experience. It's the kind of track that reminds you why you fell in love with house music in the first place: direct, physical, impossible to stand still to. Meanwhile Cloonee and Prospa have crafted something darker with "Free Your Mind," sitting at #1 on Beatport House and dominating warehouse sets across Europe. The acid breakdown is pure tension-and-release, a masterclass in how to build a moment.

The Nordic connection this week comes through Studio Barnhus – Axel Boman's "From Riches to Rags" EP on Aus Music is everything great Scandinavian house should be: warm, slightly weird, deeply musical. He's proving once again that Stockholm has a sound worth exporting. And while Full Pupp didn't have a new drop this week, Prins Thomas's influence on Norwegian disco-house continues to ripple through everything we do at Oysterdale. There's lineage there, a shared DNA of melodic, groove-focused production that doesn't chase trends.

Kevin's Pick: Route 94 – "4Get The Girl" (Defected). The bassline rolls like a freight train, the vocal chops are surgically precise, and the drop hits exactly where it should. This is peak-time house done by someone who understands the fundamentals. I've already got it cued for my next set.

Every record is a pearl. Until next week.

— Kevin / Oysterdale
