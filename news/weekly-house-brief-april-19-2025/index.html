Weekly House Brief – April 19, 2025

by Oysterdale Records

housediscoweekly-briefdefectedtoolroom

This week's house music briefing highlights new releases from Defected and Toolroom, rising artists like Rossi. and Two Shell, and the growing fusion of soulful vocals with tech-house production.

Top Releases This Week

- Jonas Rathsman – "Lego" (Defected) – Melodic house groover bridging classic piano stabs with contemporary production
- Route 94 – New Single (Defected) – UK garage/house crossover with signature 2-step rhythms
- DJ S.K.T & DJ Rae – Collaboration (Defected) – Peak-time house weapon with rolling basslines
- KC Lights, Låpsley – "Better Times" (Toolroom) – Emotive vocal house for radio and club

Artists to Watch

- Rossi. – UK's most talked-about new DJ, blending minimal and tech-house
- Two Shell – Genre-hopping duo influencing a wave of producers
- Emanuel Satie – Bridging deep house and melodic techno with growing DJ support

Label News

Defected announced their In The House Ibiza 2025 compilation with 47 tracks, while Toolroom's Miami 2025 compilation continues their investment in both established names and emerging talent.
