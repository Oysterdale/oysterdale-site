Weekly Pearls #007 — Groove Armada, Solomun x Skrillex, and the Sound of Summer

by Kevin Vadsten

Weekly Pearls #007 — Groove Armada, Solomun x Skrillex, and the Sound of Summer

There's a particular moment in summer when the light stays golden until ten, the air is thick, and the right string section hits your chest like a memory you haven't lived yet. That's the exact frequency Groove Armada dialed in this week with "Love's Theme" — a sweeping, unapologetically sentimental house cut that dropped as part of the Defected In The House Ibiza 2026 compilation. Andy Cato and Tom Findlay have been soundtracking dancefloors for over two decades, and this one proves they still know exactly where the feeling lives. If you're playing outdoors this month, this is your sunset record. No debate.

The Defected Ibiza comp itself is a 46-track statement, mixed across three discs by Andy Daniell, and it reads like a who's-who of the moment: John Summit, Honey Dijon, Rampa, ANOTR, Dam Swindle, Route 94, Eliza Rose, and more. It's the sonic equivalent of a residency at Chinois — which is exactly what Defected is running this summer, every Thursday for 23 weeks. If you want to know what house music sounds like in July 2026, this compilation is the answer.

Then there's the curveball. Solomun and Skrillex — yes, that Skrillex — dropped "Rumpta" on Diynamic this week. It's darker, more industrial, and built for warehouse rooms rather than beach bars. Skrillex has been opening his EDC Las Vegas sets with it, and the two are going b2b at Pacha Ibiza on July 5. It's not the warm, soulful territory I usually gravitate toward, but it's a fascinating signal: house is the common language now. Even bass music royalty is speaking it fluently.

Elsewhere, Traxsource is keeping the jack alive. JØRD's "Get Funky, Get Down" is exactly what the title promises — gritty, no-nonsense, and ready for the warm-up slot. And DJ Spen & Tasha LaRae's "When I Needed You Most" on Quantize is the kind of gospel-tinged, 3am record that makes a dark room feel like church. James Poole and GUDFELLA also dropped "Gator Boots" on Defected today — chunky, confident, and very poolside.

On the Nordic front, it's a quieter week for underground house specifically, but Swedish House Mafia and Lykke Li have a new collaboration arriving July 31. Details are scarce, but the teaser hints at the emotional, anthemic territory both acts do well. It's not pure house, but it's a significant Swedish moment on the global stage, and worth watching.

Kevin's Pick of the Week: Groove Armada — "Love's Theme". Because sometimes the most powerful thing a track can do is make you feel something before the kick even drops. This is the sound Oysterdale exists to celebrate. Every record is a pearl. 🦪
