This Week's Pearls – April 20, 2025

by Oysterdale Records

housediscoweekly-briefpearlsnorwegian-house

From our basement to your speakers — this week's crate dig through the house and disco underground. Spring is blooming in Oslo, and so are the releases. Here's what caught our ear while the vinyl was spinning.

Pearls From the Crates

Kameelah Waheed - Kenny Dope O'Gutta Mix
Pure NYC soulful house energy from the Traxsource charts this month. Kenny Dope brings that raw, unfiltered groove that reminds us why we fell in love with this sound in the first place. When the organ hits, you know.

DJ Spen, Reelsoul, Cleveland P. Jones - Take Me (Odyssey Inc. Remix)
Gospel-tinged soulful house that lifts you up. The kind of track that works just as well at 2 AM in a packed club as it does on a Sunday morning recovery session. This is what house music was built on — community, spirit, and undeniable groove.

Liva K - Track With The Organ (Defected)
A celebration of the legendary Korg M1 synthesizer — that iconic house piano sound that defined an era. Liva K knows her history and isn't afraid to wear those influences proudly. Released on Defected, this one proves classic sounds never go out of style.

Route 94 - 4Get The Girl
New heat on the Defected imprint. Route 94 keeps it lean, mean, and ready for the floor.

Nordic Finds 🇳🇴🇸🇪🇩🇰

Tøndra
Straight out of Finnmark, Norway's northern frontier comes this dark and danceable duo. Featured on NRK P3 Urørt, Tøndra draws inspiration from the harsh, unforgiving Arctic nature — and translates it into electronic music that hits different. This is the Nordic sound: cold environment, warm grooves. Keep your eyes on these two.

Full Pupp Records (Oslo)
Prins Thomas's label continues its mission of exploring only Norwegian talent, and we respect the dedication. With sublabels Internasjonal, Rett i Fletta, and Horisontal Mambo covering the rest of the universe, Full Pupp stays true to the local soil. Home to Todd Terje classics and the ongoing evolution of Norwegian disco house. 15+ years deep and still digging.

Beatservice Records (Tromsø)
The kitchen table label from Norway's north keeps delivering. New releases on the horizon: "Lazy Days" dropping June 2025, plus "Take Me Places" and "We're All Connected" both arriving May 2025. Artists like Boblebad, Third Attempt, and High Heeled Giants represent the Tromsø electronic scene — small city, big sounds.

Paper Recordings
25+ years of Norwegian psychedelic disco and house. The Tromsø Collective continues to push boundaries while honoring the legacy. When a label survives this long in our game, you know they're doing something right.

From the Basement

Defected is going out big — their final Croatia festival edition happens July 29 through August 3, 2026, and the lineup is complete. Meanwhile, Glitterbox is making a bold move to Amnesia Ibiza for 2026, taking over those legendary terraces every Friday from May 15 through October 9. The festival machine keeps turning.

Toolroom dropped their Leaders Of The New School 2025 compilation in February, continuing their mission to spotlight emerging talent. They sit at #2 on Beatport's House charts (with Defected at #1, naturally). Some things are constants in this business.

Back home in Norway, the electronic scene feels vital. From Tøndra's Arctic darkness to Full Pupp's disco optimism, there's a breadth of sound happening here that the world should know about. That's part of why we started Oysterdale — to help share these pearls.

Kevin's Pick of the Week

Liva K - Track With The Organ

This one's speaking to me personally. That Korg M1 organ sound takes me back to the foundational records that made me want to produce house music in the first place. There's something about revisiting those classic synth patches with modern production sensibilities that just works. Liva K isn't just copying the past — she's carrying the torch forward. When that bassline drops under the organ chords... that's the moment. That's why we do this.
