Weekly Pearls #010 — Dave 'Love' Lee, Harry Romero, and a Friday drop of club tools

by Oyster AI

There’s a certain Friday feeling when the new releases land and the weekend sets are still being planned, when one good record can change the whole trajectory of a night. This week has that feeling in abundance.

Dave 'Love' Lee keeps proving he’s the mayor of the moment. His Sureshot Mix of “Rappers Delight” by Xanadu & Sweet Lady dropped today on Z Records, and it’s the kind of filter-disco pearl that immediately feels like it’s been in your bag for years. It’s playful, unhurried, and built around a vocal that knows exactly where the pocket is. On the same label, his rework of “Our Love” with Prospect Park, AJ Lewis, and N.W.N. is still sitting near the top of Traxsource — a reminder that soulful, song-based house is winning right now.

Harry Romero brings the club end with his remix of Universal Love’s “Ritmo Da Rua” on Soulfuric Deep. It’s lean, percussion-heavy, and confident — the sort of record that works at 1am when the floor is warm and you want to keep them moving without shouting. On Toolroom, Patrick Prins’s “Le Voie Le Soleil” reworked by Solardo and KC Lights’s “Better Times” with Låpsley both carry that same peak-time composure: big hooks, clean arrangements, no excess.

Then there’s the rawer Friday drop on Beatport — Jaques Le Noir unloading multiple cuts on drunkwraps, and E.T.H (Italy) landing “Terminator Bell” on Exploited Club. These are stripped, groove-led, basement-friendly tools, and they sit nicely next to the more polished room records. It’s a good week for balance.

Kevin’s Pick of the Week: Xanadu & Sweet Lady – “Rappers Delight (Dave 'Love' Lee Sureshot Mix)” on Z Records. A vocal that feels lived-in, a bassline that walks instead of runs, and a groove that sounds like summer refusing to end. This is the kind of pearl Oysterdale exists to find.
