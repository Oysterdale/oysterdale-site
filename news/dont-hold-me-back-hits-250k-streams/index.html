Don't Hold Me Back Hits 250K Streams

by Oysterdale Records

It's a milestone worth celebrating. MINIJACK's "Don't Hold Me Back" — featuring the incredible vocals of Emél — has just crossed 250,000 streams.

A quarter of a million plays. That's not just a number. It's a quarter million moments where someone pressed play, felt the groove, and kept it moving. From late-night studio sessions to morning commutes, from packed dancefloors to headphone journeys — this track has found its way into all of them.

Released as a follow-up to Emél's debut "Get On Up" with OKPLUS, "Don't Hold Me Back" proved that the first one wasn't a fluke. The track has been championed by DJs across the scene and continues to find new ears every single day.

What makes this one special? It's that rare blend of groove and soul. MINIJACK's polished production provides the foundation, but it's Emél's vocal that carries the emotion — that feeling of release, of letting go, of dancing like nobody's watching. That's what house music is about.

To everyone who's streamed, shared, played, and danced to this one: thank you. The journey is far from over.
