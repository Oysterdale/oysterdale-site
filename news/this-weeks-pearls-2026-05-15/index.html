This Week's Pearls – 2026-05-15

by Oysterdale Records

house disco weekly-brief pearls

It's been a heavy week in the house world, and Friday feels like the right moment to pause and sort through what matters. Two massive Defected drops today – Moodymann remixing Dames Brown & Amp Fiddler, and Deetron getting the House Masters treatment – remind me why this label has been the gold standard for so long. They don't chase trends. They curate culture.

Moodymann's touch on "Take Me As I Am" is exactly what you'd expect: slow, hypnotic, deeply Detroit. The Rhodes breathe, the vocal floats, and suddenly you're in a dark room at 3am remembering why you started caring about this music. Meanwhile Deetron's compilation is a masterclass in building a career without compromise – years of underground respect, now validated by the biggest platform in house. That's the long game, and it's the game Oysterdale wants to play.

The Nordic thread this week comes through Studio Barnhus. Axel Boman's "From Riches to Rags" EP on Aus Music is warm, slightly weird, completely Swedish in the best way. House with personality, not algorithms. And while Full Pupp didn't have new vinyl this week, Prins Thomas's influence runs through everything – his sound is in the water here in Oslo, whether people know it or not.

Kevin's Pick: Dames Brown & Amp Fiddler – "Take Me As I Am (Moodymann Remix)" (Defected). It's not the obvious choice – it's not a peak-time banger. But it's perfect. The way Kenny Dixon Jr. stretches time, lets the vocal breathe, builds tension without ever releasing it conventionally – that's artistry. This is the track I'll be listening to on repeat this weekend, studying how the greats do it.

Every record is a pearl. Until next week.

— Kevin / Oysterdale
