OKPLUS

OKPLUS is a Norwegian disco-house trio blending live instrumentation, vintage groove and modern club energy. Their sound is warm, danceable and rich in both musicianship and emotion. Releases include both original productions and remixes — always grounded in funk, soul and rhythm.
