Emél is a Norwegian-Bosnian artist and songwriter known for her distinctive voice, songwriting skills, and natural sense of groove. Some voices don't need much introduction — hers is one of them. There's a clarity and presence in the way she sings that immediately cuts through, whether it's on a stripped-back record or a full club production. She first made a strong impression with Get On Up, her collaboration with OKPLUS — one of the early releases on Oysterdale Records. The track quickly gained traction on Traxsource, earning support from DJs across the house and disco scene and establishing her as a voice to watch. Her follow-up, Don't Hold Me Back with MINIJACK, showed that the debut was no one-off — continuing to build momentum and reaching over 200,000 streams. As both a writer and performer, Emél brings a balance of soulfulness and control. It's that combination — musicality, authenticity, and instinct — that defines her as an artist. Now part of the Oysterdale family, she represents the core of what the label stands for: timeless records, real feeling, and music that lasts beyond the moment.
