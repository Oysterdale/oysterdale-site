  Art Of Tones is the project of French DJ and producer Ludovic Llorca, also known through his long-running artist project Llorca.

  Over the years, Ludovic has established himself as a respected producer, remixer and DJ, with releases and remixes spanning a wide range of labels and artists.

  Art Of Tones joined the Oysterdale catalogue with his remix of OKPLUS & Emél's "Get On Up".
