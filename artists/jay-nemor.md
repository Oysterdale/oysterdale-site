bio: |-
  Jay Nemor (Jason Nemor Harden) is an American singer, songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist, born in Houston, Texas and now based in Gothenburg, Sweden.

  He started out on electric bass at the age of nine, moved on to alto saxophone, and was playing in stage and concert bands through his early teens. A childhood concert with Earth, Wind & Fire sparked the dream, and his sound is rooted in the classic soul of Marvin Gaye, Donny Hathaway, Curtis Mayfield, Bill Withers and Stevie Wonder.

  A professional basketball career took him to Iceland in the late 90s, where he rediscovered music and found his way to the microphone. Since then he has worked as a session vocalist, saxophonist, songwriter and producer across soul, R&B and deep house, releasing music on labels including King Street Sounds, Do It Now Recordings and Urban Orange Music — always delivered in his signature bass-baritone voice.
