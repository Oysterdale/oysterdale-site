bio: |-
  Sebastian Emes is a Norwegian DJ and producer, known for capturing immediate emotion in his music, a quality that has become his signature sound.

  His breakout single “Making Me (Feel Good)” has surpassed 2.5 million Spotify streams and received global support, including a premiere by house legend David Penn on his Urbana radioshow. With releases on labels such as Peppermint Jam, Emes continues to build strong momentum.

  His collaboration “With You” with Swiss producer Doobious has received ongoing airplay on Switzerland’s Radio SRF 3 and Sirius XM, supported by Liquid Todd. His track “Really Nice” (Hive) reached #18 on the Traxsource Top 100 Deep House Chart. He has also co-produced the platinum-certified single “KlubbKamel” for Norwegian artist Kamelen.

  Emes’ music has been featured by leading music blogs and editorial playlists, including Disco Belle and Dancing Astronaut.

  A 2x Red Bull 3Style champion, he brings over 15 years of experience as a full-time DJ. He has performed extensively across Norway’s festival and club scene, as well as internationally in Australia, the US, and throughout Europe.

  He has shared the stage with a wide range of international artists, including The Shapeshifters, Dave Lee, Breakbot & Irfane, DJ Jazzy Jeff, Big Boi, Karpe, Matoma, J. Cole, Kartell, 99 Souls, Dagny, Mr. Belt & Wezol, NEIKED, and KREAM.
