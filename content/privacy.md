**Last updated: September 2025**

Oysterdale Records respects your privacy. This website is a simple, informational site and we do not collect or store any personal data directly.  

## Cookies and Third-Party Services
- We do not set cookies ourselves.  
- We use third-party embeds such as **Spotify** to provide music playback. These services may place cookies in your browser for functionality, analytics, or personalization.  
- You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.  

## Data Collection
We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts on this site.  
If you contact us via email, we will only use your information to reply to your inquiry.  

## Your Rights
If you are based in the EU/EEA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we may hold about you (e.g., from direct email correspondence).
