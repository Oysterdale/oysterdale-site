There’s a moment around the last hour of a sunset set when the light is almost gone, the crowd is loose, and the right vocal can make the whole room feel like they’re breathing together. This week belongs to those records.

**Chasing Kurt** have finally let *From The Inside* loose in the wild again, and Reznik’s extended remix has turned it into a terrace anthem. The piano pads in like it’s in no hurry, the bassline rolls underneath like a slow tide, and Lukas Polocze’s vocal waits until the room is ready before it leans in. It’s out today on Defected, and it already feels like one of those tracks that will define the back end of the summer.

Over on Toolroom, **Ferreck Dawn** is back with *Lost At The End*, a peak-time cut built around a huge bassline and the hypnotic voice of **Darla Jade**. Ferreck has spent the last decade proving he can make house music that works in a festival tent and a basement at 4am, and this one does both without breaking a sweat. It’s polished, yes, but there’s a human pulse in it that keeps it from ever feeling sterile.

Defected has been on a serious run. **Melé** dropped *Soul Makossa* last Friday, all Afro-Latin percussion and hands-in-the-air energy, and Detroit pioneer **Stacey Pullen** made his DFTD debut with *I Wanna Dance*, a lean, jack-heavy record that reminds you house and techno have always been cousins. Add the *Defected In The House Ibiza 2026* compilation still doing damage at Chinois every Thursday, and the label is essentially providing the soundtrack to July.

Toolroom Radio 850 landed on 12 July with **Danny Rhys**, **Ferreck Dawn** and **Roland Clark** behind the decks, and Clark’s voice showed up everywhere: on Tenacious’ *Alright*, on HoneyLuv’s *This Is My Life*, and woven through the show’s DNA. Some vocalists are just part of the furniture of house music; Clark is one of them.

On the Nordic side, there’s a genuine signal this week. **Of Norway** — the Norwegian duo working in melodic, deep, emotional house — landed on Beatport’s *On Our Radar 2026: House* chart with *August Hush*. It’s a quiet win, but it matters: Scandinavian melodic house is still breaking through global radar, and that’s a lane Oysterdale understands well.

**Kevin’s Pick of the Week:** *Chasing Kurt – From The Inside (Reznik Extended Remix)*. A vocal that waits for its moment, a piano that earns every chord, and a groove that feels like the last hour of a sunset set. This is the kind of pearl Oysterdale exists to find.
