Been digging through the crates this week and there's a serious groove in the air. Defected is honoring Deetron with a House Masters compilation that reminds us why the Swiss producer remains untouchable — eight tracks of pure, unfiltered deep house that sound just as good in a basement as they do at a festival. Meanwhile Ferreck Dawn keeps proving he's one of the most consistent voices in funky house right now. "Say What You Mean" has that effortless, rolling bassline that just works.

Over at Toolroom, Crusy and Jem Cooke linked up for "Good To Go" — a proper summer anthem with vocals that stick. It's the kind of track that makes you want to dig out your old disco edits and see what else might fit. Speaking of which, I keep coming back to that Oden & Fatzo cut with Barney Bones on Defected. Laid-back, bass-heavy, vocal-led — exactly the lane I want Oysterdale swimming in.

**Kevin's Pick:** Ferreck Dawn – "Say What You Mean" — There's something about how this track sits in a mix. Not demanding attention, but impossible to ignore once it's rolling. That's the sweet spot.
