It's Friday, the sun is finally sticking around in Oslo, and the basement sessions are stretching later into the evening. This week feels like summer is actually arriving, and the releases are matching that energy perfectly.

Solardo's "Forever" dropped today on Defected, and it's the kind of record that reminds you why you fell in love with house music in the first place. Sabrina Johnston's vocal is pure church – spiritual, soaring, undeniable. Over a rolling bassline and tight percussion, it bridges classic house with modern peak-time energy. The Manchester duo knows exactly what they're doing, and Defected knew exactly what they were signing.

Last week's pearls are still echoing too. Moodymann's remix of Dames Brown & Amp Fiddler continues to reveal new details on every listen – the way Kenny Dixon Jr. stretches time, lets the Rhodes breathe, gives the vocal space to float. It's not a track you dance to aggressively; it's a track you surrender to. Meanwhile Deetron's House Masters compilation validates years of underground respect with the biggest platform in house. That's the long game, and it's the game Oysterdale wants to play.

The Nordic thread is quieter this week – no major releases from Studio Barnhus or Full Pupp – but that's okay. Not every week needs Scandinavian headlines. The foundations are solid, and summer festival season is approaching. Something's brewing.

**Kevin's Pick:** Solardo – "Forever" feat. Sabrina Johnston (Defected). It's out TODAY, and it's everything great house music should be: spiritual, physical, timeless. Sabrina Johnston's voice does what only the greats can do – it elevates the room. I've already got it cued for my next set, and I have a feeling it'll be there all summer.
