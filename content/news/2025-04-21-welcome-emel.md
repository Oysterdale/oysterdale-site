Some voices stop you in your tracks. The kind that makes you put down the coffee, turn up the speakers, and just listen. That's exactly what happened the first time we heard Emél.

We're proud to announce that Emél has officially joined the Oysterdale family. This Norwegian-Bosnian artist brings something we deeply believe in: soulfulness, creativity, and that rare ability to lift a room with just her voice.

Truth is, Emél has been part of our story from the beginning. Her debut **"Get On Up"** — a collaboration with **OKPLUS** — was one of the first releases on Oysterdale, and what a way to start. The track became a massive hit on Traxsource, earning support from heavyweights like **Michael Gray, Dave Lee, Sgt. Slick, Da Lukas, Dr. Packer, N.W.N., Birdee** and many more. The **Art Of Tones remix** has been sitting pretty in the Top 100 Soul/Funk/Disco charts for over 3 months now.

Then came **"Don't Hold Me Back"** — produced together with **MINIJACK** — her follow-up that proved the first one wasn't a fluke. That track has now streamed more than **200,000 times** and keeps finding new ears every day.

Emél represents exactly why we started Oysterdale. Not just to release records, but to find artists who understand that every track matters. Every record is a pearl.

Stay tuned — we're just getting started.
