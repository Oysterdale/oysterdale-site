There’s a particular hour in a summer set when the light is almost gone, the crowd is loose, and the right vocal can make the whole room feel like they’re breathing together. This week belongs to those records.

**Oden & Fatzo** and **Noa Milee** finally let *“You Don't Want To Think About It”* loose today on Defected, and it understands that hour completely. The French trio keeps the kick soft and the bassline patient; Noa Milee’s vocal waits until the groove has earned its space, then leans in like a secret. It’s the kind of restrained, soulful house that doesn’t beg for attention — it earns it, one phrase at a time.

Defected is on a serious run. **Chasing Kurt**’s *“From The Inside”* — reworked by **Reznik** into an extended terrace anthem — is still climbing after landing last Friday. Piano pads roll in like they have nowhere else to be, Lukas Polocze’s voice finds its moment, and the whole thing feels like the last hour of a sunset set. Add **Melé**’s Afro-Latin *“Soul Makossa”* and Detroit pioneer **Stacey Pullen**’s DFTD debut *“I Wanna Dance”*, and the label is essentially providing the soundtrack to July.

Over on Traxsource, the top spot this week belongs to **Prospect Park**, **Dave 'Love' Lee**, **AJ Lewis**, and **N.W.N.** with *“Our Love (N.W.N. Remix)”* on Z Records. It’s filtered disco-house with a vocal that lifts without shouting — the kind of remix that makes the original feel like it always belonged on a dancefloor. Right behind it, **Ethan Gray** and **Andre Espeut**’s *“Give Me Time”* on Sultra Records proves that soulful restraint still sells, and **Jay Vegas**’ *“Makin' Luv”* on Hot Stuff is a peak-time jackin’ hook that does exactly what the title promises.

Toolroom is keeping the club end sharp. **KC Lights** and **Låpsley**’s *“Better Times (Club Edit)”*, **CASSIMM** and **Bruno Blanc**’s *“House Affair”*, and **Mark Knight** and **James Hurr**’s take on *“Lady (Hear Me Tonight)”* are all in rotation, while the music video for **Crusy** and **Jem Cooke**’s *“Good To Go”* is out now. Fabric and Toronto events are on the calendar too — the label is moving like summer is its season.

This week’s Nordic pearl comes from Swedish label **Studio Barnhus**. **Axel Boman**’s *“Space Drag”* has been reworked by Danish producer **Kasper Bjørke**, and the two versions — *“Acid Left & Right”* and *“Space Drag (Kasper Bjørke Acid Dub Mix)”* — are architectural, acid-tinged, and quietly melancholy. It’s a reminder that Scandinavian melodic house still carries global weight, and that a well-placed remix can turn a 2024 original into a 2026 club moment. No new Norwegian release this week, but **Todd Terje** plays Belfast on Saturday — a live signal worth watching.

**Kevin’s Pick of the Week:** *Chasing Kurt – “From The Inside (Reznik Extended Remix)”* on Defected. A vocal that waits for its moment, a piano that earns every chord, and a groove that feels like the last hour of a sunset set. This is the kind of pearl Oysterdale exists to find.
