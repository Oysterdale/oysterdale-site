September arrives with its sleeves rolled up. The summer glow is fading into something harder and more focused, and this week's pearls carry that shift — big-room drums, disco shimmer, and one announcement that says a lot about where house is heading.

CASSIMM is the name on everyone's lips this week, and he earns it twice over. His new single **"Paradise"** lands on Toolroom today, and he's hosting their flagship House Paradise mix around it — a record built on rolling percussion and a vocal hook that knows exactly when to arrive. The boy is everywhere, and so is his sound. Watch, too, his remix of Nico De Andrea and Baron's **"Kingston Groove"** on Armada, which is quietly becoming the most talked-about ID in the sets that matter.

The disco pulse is alive and well. **Claptone & The Mekanism** team up with Jorge Bezerra for **"Doing The Disco"** on Exploited, and it is exactly what the title promises — playful, warm, and impossible to stand still to. **Alaia & Gallo**'s **"Lipstick"** gets the Tony Romera treatment on Toolroom, turning a soulful daytime groover into something that can hold a peak-time floor. Romera himself is having a moment — a Toolroom single on the way plus music lined up on Solid Grooves and Insomniac.

Defected made the week's biggest noise without dropping a single record. They announced **Melé's** debut album ***Alive On Arrival*** — fourteen tracks, a double vinyl landing October 2 — and then, a day later, announced a partnership with the Aston Martin F1 team. Think about that for a second: a house label now sits next to Formula 1. The music we love keeps proving it's a culture, not a category.

For the heads: Fred Everything and Teuteu's **"Arret Infini Pt. 1"** from the Beatport Weekend Picks is a masterclass in patience — dubby, unhurried, deep. And SOULSTAR, the little soulful label that could, floods this week's Traxsource mix rotation with Vikter Duplaix, Dawn Tallman and a Kai Alce remix. Proof that taste, consistently applied, beats budget every time.

Nordic notes are quiet this week — no major placements or releases from our corner of the world in the last seven days. Sometimes the scene takes a breath. We'll be listening when it exhales.

**Kevin's Pick of the Week:** Claptone & The Mekanism ft. Jorge Bezerra — **"Doing The Disco."** It is pure Oysterdale DNA: a disco heartbeat, a wink in its eye, and a groove that makes strangers dance like old friends. Some records chase the moment. This one is the moment.

Every record is a pearl. See you on the floor.
