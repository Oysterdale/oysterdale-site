# Weekly Pearls — June 19, 2026

The nights are getting shorter up here in Oslo, but the grooves are getting deeper. While the rest of the city counts down to summer vacation, we've been locked in, sifting through the week's finest house music so you don't have to.

This week's haul is a proper mixed bag: a Dutch duo making serious waves, a Defected newcomer with a throwback sound that hits just right, and the annual Ibiza compilation that always manages to soundtrack the season perfectly.

---

**ANOTR** have done it again. "Talk To You" — the lead single from their third album *Withness* — has officially become *the* song of summer 2026. It's sitting at #1 on Beatport, #7 on Shazam's global chart, and has racked up over 405 million views across user-generated content. The Dutch duo have this rare gift for making records that work at 3am in a warehouse *and* on your Sunday morning commute. The full album, out now on their own NO ART imprint, features Wayne Snow, TEED, and more. It's deep, it's soulful, and it's going to be everywhere.

Then there's **Adam Ten**, an Israeli producer who just landed on Defected with "I Never Knew." It's a throwback house heater in the best possible way — '90s energy, modern production, and a groove that doesn't quit. At 125 BPM, it's one of those records that finds its way into your set and refuses to leave. The forums are already buzzing.

Speaking of Defected, their annual **Defected In The House Ibiza 2026** compilation drops June 26, compiled by Andy Daniell. The sampler landed this week and it's got us itching for the White Isle. **Piem** features with "Activated (I Just Wanna)" — a bright, infectious cut that screams poolside at sunset. Piem's also just released the "Potato Head EP" on snackwax, making this a banner month for the Spanish producer.

**Makèz & LYMA** round out our picks with "Come Back Tomorrow" — a peak-time vocal house gem that's already getting festival rotation. Dutch production, soulful delivery, and that Defected polish. This one feels like a breakout.

---

## Kevin's Pick of the Week

**Adam Ten – "I Never Knew"**

There's something about this record that keeps pulling me back. Maybe it's the nostalgic synth stabs, maybe it's the way the groove locks in and doesn't let go. It reminds me of why I fell in love with house music in the first place — that feeling when a track drops and the room becomes one thing. This is going straight into my next DJ set. If you're playing anywhere this summer, you need this in your bag.
