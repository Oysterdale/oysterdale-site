There's a particular moment, somewhere around 3am on a terrace with the sea at your back, when a track hits just right and time stops. This week's pearls are built for that moment.

**Prospa** have done something rare with *Free Your Mind*. The Leeds duo's debut album – also the first full-length release on CircoLoco Records – doesn't chase trends. It synthesizes them. Euphoric house, rave nostalgia, breakbeat experimentation, and peak-time club energy all threaded into one cohesive arc. DJ Mag called it "sleek, chrome-edged, contemporary," but what they didn't say is how it feels. "Let the Music" is gospel-house soul for Ibiza terraces, built with patience and purpose. When those piano chords hit, you remember why you fell in love with this music. This is the benchmark: modern production, timeless emotion, unapologetically house.

Then there's **Roger Sanchez**, returning with *Spectrum* – his first body of work in twenty years. Twenty years. That's not a comeback, that's a statement of permanence. The album features "Grinnin'" with Fedde Le Grand, "Temptation" with Low Steppa, and the focus track "How Do We Say Goodbye" with Karen Harding. Classic vocal house, delivered by someone who never forgot what the genre is for. He's heavy on the Ibiza circuit this summer – Pacha's Flower Power residency plus festival dates across Europe – proving that consistency and authenticity still open doors.

**Makèz** took over Defected Radio Show 517 this week, and it wasn't a guest slot – it was a statement. The Dutch duo's "Come Back Tomorrow" with LYMA is already moving floors, but the full takeover showed their range: Detroit Rising remixes, Louie Vega reworks, Life on Planets collaborations. Defected is investing in them, and they're repaying that faith with emotional, peak-time house that never forgets the groove. The Toronto takeover on July 11 at Sunnyside Pavilion is next on their radar.

On **Toolroom**, Mark Knight and James Hurr linked with Cutty Ranks for "Bad Man" – a dancefloor bomb that fuses house weight with dancehall authority. Knight's celebrating his birthday at Uva Wynwood in Miami on July 20, and if history is any guide, that room will be packed with people who know exactly what they're there for. Also worth noting: Jude & Frank's "The Sound Of House" on Toolroom Trax, keeping the label's foundational sound alive and current.

Finally, a quiet signal from the north: **Of Norway** landed on Beatport's June Best New Hype – House chart with "August Hush" on Connaisseur Recordings. Deep, melodic, 126 BPM, extended version running past six minutes. Proper DJ tool material. No major festival announcements, no label signings – just a Norwegian producer finding his floor in the global charts. That's enough. That's how it starts.

**Kevin's Pick:** Prospa – "Let the Music"

The whole album is a masterclass in tension and release, but this track – with its preaching vocals and steady, patient build – is the pearl in the shell. For Oysterdale, this is the target: music that doesn't need to explain itself. It just moves you.
