# Weekly Pearls — 29 May 2026

There's a particular frequency that house music hits when the vocals are real, the groove is patient, and the room is dark enough that you stop thinking and start feeling. This week had that frequency in spades.

Jonas Blue dropped "Girl" on Defected today — a track that knows exactly what it wants to do and does it without apology. Big room, big emotion, hands in the air. It's the kind of record that reminds you why melodic house still matters when it's done with conviction. Following "Edge Of Desire," Jonas Blue is proving he understands the architecture of a summer anthem.

But the real pearl this week came from a quieter place. George Smeddles, the House Gospel Choir, and Chez Damier came together on PIV Records for the *Angels EP*, and it's the kind of release that makes you stop mid-task and listen properly. Gospel vocals over a house groove shouldn't feel novel in 2026, but when it's done this well — with space, with reverence, with a bassline that moves like it has somewhere to be — it feels like a reminder. House music was built on voices like these. The title track is the obvious entry point, but "Feel It" with Chez Damier is where the EP earns its keep. Deep, rolling, and unhurried.

Solardo brought "Forever" to DFTD, and it's exactly what you'd expect from the Manchester duo: tight, driving, festival-ready. The vocal hook sits on top of the percussion like it belongs there. Meanwhile, Deetron's *House Masters* compilation dropped on Classic Music Company, a definitive collection of his work for Defected. It's not new material, but it's a masterclass in what happens when techno precision meets house soul.

Toolroom kept the pressure on with Alex Preston's "By The Hour" and DJ Dove's "Lonely No More" — both tracks that understand the current appetite for melodic, accessible house that still works in a club. Crusy and Jem Cooke's "Good To Go" continues to circulate, and rightly so. The Spanish producer is having a moment.

## Kevin's Pick of the Week

**George Smeddles & House Gospel Choir — "Angels"**

I'm picking this because it represents something I want Oysterdale to keep reaching for: the intersection of real human voices and dancefloor utility. There's a tendency in house music right now to hide behind loops and samples, and this track does the opposite. It puts the voice front and center, builds the groove around it, and trusts the room to meet it halfway. That's the gamble worth taking.

## What's Moving

Glitterbox is now in its third week at Amnesia, and the line-up is staggering: Armand Van Helden, Honey Dijon, Masters At Work, David Morales, Todd Terry, Derrick Carter, Dimitri From Paris. If you're in Ibiza this summer, Fridays are spoken for. Defected, meanwhile, continues its World Cup 2026 merchandise push — a reminder that the best labels don't just release music, they build culture around it.

On the Scandinavian front, Todd Terje played Copenhagen this week (May 23) at Klub Werkstatt. No new release, but the man's presence alone is enough to keep the Norwegian disco flag flying. We're watching for any studio activity — when Terje moves, the world listens.

Every record is a pearl.
