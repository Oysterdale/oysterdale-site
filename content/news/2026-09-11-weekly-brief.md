There's a particular feeling when a record from your own corner of the world lands somewhere it belongs. This week it happened — and it set the tone for everything else we found.

Fisher's rework of Bob Sinclar's "World Hold On" is sitting at #1 on Beatport's House chart, and it's easy to hear why. The 2006 anthem gets the full modern treatment — bigger drums, wider skies, that same lift-off moment intact. Set-closers don't come more guaranteed this season.

Then there's the pearl we're most proud of. **N.W.N., straight out of our own Norwegian network, have landed "No More Games" featuring Emél on Soulfuric Deep** — and Australia's Husky has already reached for it in his Bobbin Headcast. Soulful, deep, and exactly where the sound is heading. When friends make records this good on imprints this respected, the whole circle rises. We're celebrating this one loudly.

Defected has a big one on the horizon too: Melé's long-awaited debut album *Alive On Arrival* arrives 25 September — a 14-track, collaboration-heavy statement with Jords, Wonky Logic, RIA and IRAH. The lead single "Body Move" is already out, and the full rollout is a masterclass in how to build an album in this scene. Worth studying for anyone planning a bigger project. Meanwhile, Defected Malta's 2026 boat party schedule just dropped — five boats across three days and a floating venue called The Island. House music all life long, indeed.

Toolroom keeps feeding the next wave with *Leaders Of The New School 2026 Vol. 2* on Toolroom Trax — eighteen tracks deep, with CASSIMM's *House Paradise* mix painting the current landscape in broad strokes. And keep an ear on Saoirse's 'Boombox', which Mixmag flagged this week as one of the cuts to watch.

### Kevin's Pick of the Week

**N.W.N. ft. Emél — "No More Games" (Soulfuric Deep).** No contest this week. A Norwegian act on a soulful institution, supported internationally within days of release. This is what building a scene looks like — one pearl at a time.
