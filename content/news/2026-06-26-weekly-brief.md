There's a particular feeling when summer hits its stride and the compilations start landing. You know the ones — the records that soundtrack the warm nights, the long drives, the moments when the room locks in and nothing else matters. This week, Defected delivered theirs. And a few other pearls surfaced worth your attention.

The big news is the **Defected In The House Ibiza 2026** compilation — 46 tracks spanning triple vinyl and CD, featuring the heavy hitters you'd expect (ANOTR, Rampa, Honey Dijon, Groove Armada, Dam Swindle) alongside the kind of careful curation that reminds you why Defected still defines the sound of summer. If you're playing anywhere near the Mediterranean in the coming months — or just want to close your eyes and pretend you are — this is essential listening.

Over on Traxsource, **DJ PP** is sitting at #1 with "Kürüf," a track that does exactly what its title suggests: it grinds, it grooves, it doesn't let up. Peak-time energy for the 4AM crew. Shermanology lands nearby on the charts with "Insane" — and the name is no exaggeration. This is raw, stripped-back, devastating club music. The kind of record that clears the floor of pretenders and leaves only the true believers.

Defected also has new singles from **ClÜb De Combat** ("All The Single Ladies," out today) and the pairing of **Makèz & LYMA** from earlier in the month. Both worth your time. Toolroom answers with their own Ibiza compilation, while Mixmag's Burn Energy Tour is surfacing emerging DJs across Europe and North Africa — always worth watching for names before they break.

No major Nordic signal this week, though the Scandinavian festival circuit is in full swing — Solvesborg, Trondheim, Aarhus, Stavanger all running. Sometimes the absence of news is news itself: the scene is out dancing, not releasing.

## Kevin's Pick

**Shermanology — "Insane"**

There's a moment in every great set where you drop a track and the room just stops. Not stops dancing — stops breathing. Then the groove locks back in and everything is better than before. "Insane" is that track. Raw, unapologetic, and built for the kind of nights that become stories. This is why we do what we do.
